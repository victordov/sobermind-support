Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. SoberMind has no accounts, and nothing you enter is ever uploaded. The app sends only anonymous usage counts (which features are used, never what you write or log; you can turn this off in Settings) and the App Store record of a purchase; the privacy policy lists exactly what. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, those backups include SoberMind’s data, as they do for your other apps; they are yours, and we can’t see them.

How do I move to a new phone?

Settings → Backup (JSON) to export a file, then Restore from Backup on the new phone. Backup, restore and CSV export are free and always will be.

I lost my data / deleted the app

Deleting the app deletes its data, and your entries are on no server to recover them from. If you have a backup file, Restore from Backup will bring it all back. Restoring your whole iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup also brings the app’s data back as it was when that backup was made.

How do I delete all my data?

Settings → Erase All Data removes everything SoberMind keeps on your iPhone and starts you from the beginning. It can’t be undone, so save a backup first if you might want anything later. Backup files you shared and your own device backups are not touched.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings → SoberMind Premium → Manage Subscription, or in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Canceling keeps Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

I paid but Premium is not showing

Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

Can I change my quit date?

Yes: Settings → Quit Date. Badges you have earned stay earned; moving the date never removes them.

I slipped. Did I lose everything?

No. Log it honestly: the counter on Home starts again from your last drink, and your alcohol-free days, badges and history all stay. If you have a streak freeze and a streak worth protecting, it is used automatically. The counter is not a verdict.

The widget shows nothing

Open the app once after installing; the widget reads a snapshot the app writes. If it still shows placeholder data, remove and re-add the widget.

Notifications

They are off unless you turned them on. Settings → Notifications, where you can also set the time or turn on Discreet Notifications so the wording never mentions sobriety on your lock screen.

Not medical care

SoberMind is a self-tracking and education tool, not medical treatment. Stopping heavy daily drinking suddenly can cause serious withdrawal. If you feel shaky, confused or unwell, contact a doctor or your local emergency service.
